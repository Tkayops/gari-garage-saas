Everything a garage actually does

From the moment a car rolls in to the moment the customer pays.

Digital job cards

Check in a car with its complaint, mileage, fuel level and items left inside. Every status change is time-stamped on a timeline.

Customer approves the quote

Send an itemised quote link on WhatsApp. The approved total is locked, and extra work needs approval again before the car can be released.

Stock that matches the shelf

Parts are deducted when they go on a job and returned if a line is removed. Low-stock alerts and a full movement history for every part.

Jobs for each mechanic

Assign work, see who is doing what, and keep prices and costs hidden from mechanics. They only see the jobs given to them.

Invoices and payments

16% VAT invoices, part payments, and M-Pesa codes checked and never accepted twice. A field to record the KRA eTIMS invoice number.

Reports that show the money

Collected, owed, gross profit, turnaround time, mechanic output and your best-earning parts.
